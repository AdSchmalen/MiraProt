# MiraProt Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MiraProt aims to provide a respectful and constructive environment for people who use, discuss, or contribute to the project.

Participation is welcome regardless of background, experience level, identity, career stage, institution, or field of work.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- discuss ideas, code, methods, and evidence rather than attacking people
- give and receive technical or scientific criticism constructively
- respect differences in experience and perspective
- avoid publishing another person's private or sensitive information
- help keep Issues and Pull Requests relevant and useful

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, or intimidation
- discriminatory or derogatory comments
- personal attacks or repeated hostile behavior
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- deliberate disruption of project discussions
- publishing private information without permission
- other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome

## Scientific disagreement

Disagreement about software design, statistics, bioinformatics, or scientific interpretation is not itself a conduct violation.

Strong technical criticism is acceptable when it remains focused on methods, evidence, code, or reproducibility and is expressed respectfully.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The project maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments, Issues, Pull Requests, or other contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary.

Decisions will be made with the aim of keeping the project constructive, safe, and focused.

## Reporting conduct concerns

If a concern can safely be discussed publicly, it may be raised in the relevant GitHub Issue or Pull Request.

For behavior that violates GitHub's platform policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting tools.

MiraProt does not currently publish a private project-specific conduct-reporting email address. Please do not use the security vulnerability reporting channel for ordinary conduct disputes.

This reporting section may be expanded if the project develops a larger contributor community or a dedicated private project contact becomes available.
